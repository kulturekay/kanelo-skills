# Landing Pages, Funnels, and VSLs

> **Client Acquisition · File 2.**
> *Inbound Leads — Personal Branding* (File 1) and *Sign Clients Like Never Before* generate **attention** — through content and outreach. But attention needs somewhere to go and a system to convert it. This file is the **conversion infrastructure**: the landing pages, the funnels that route traffic to the right next step, the VSL that does the selling, and the automation that makes sure no lead falls through the cracks.

The core idea: **traffic without a funnel leaks.** Every visitor should land on a page built for one job, be routed to a conversion step matched to how warm they are, and — whether they convert or not — be captured by automation that either nurtures them or retargets them.

## 2. Traffic sources

Two engines feed the funnel — both built in earlier files:

- **Social media content** — organic content from your personal brand (*Inbound Leads — Personal Branding*).
- **Outreach Email/DM** — cold and hot outreach (*Sign Clients Like Never Before*) and relationship DMs (*Inbound Leads*).

They converge on **Outreach Email/DM** as the central routing node, then branch into the entry points below.

## 3. The five funnel entry points

Each entry point is a different "next step" you can route a lead into. They range from **low-commitment** (give an email for a freebie) to **high-commitment** (book a call now) — match the entry point to how warm the lead is.

| # | Entry point | Mechanism | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Webinar opt-in** | A free training pitched as **available for [X] time only** — *not* framed as a live event. Captures name + email. ("The event is 100% free — all I want is your name and email.") | Building a list + warming cold-to-mid leads with urgency |
| 2 | **Calendly link (direct)** | Book a call straight away | The hottest leads, ready to talk |
| 3 | **Get email/WhatsApp + set a time** | Capture contact and manually schedule | Warm leads in conversation (e.g. via DM) |
| 4 | **Landing page with VSL** | A video sales letter does the selling, then a button to book a call ("CREATORS, SCALE YOUR BRAND TO 8 FIGURES A YEAR → JOIN NOW") | Mid-warm leads who need convincing before a call |
| 5 | **Squeeze page lead magnet** | A free toolkit/training (e.g. "Business Toolkit — 5-Figure Sales Process") in exchange for an email, feeding an email nurture sequence | Cold leads — capture now, sell later |

> **Principle:** don't send everyone to the same place. A cold stranger isn't booking a call — send them to the lead magnet. A hot DM conversation doesn't need a VSL — send the Calendly link. The entry point is a function of lead temperature.

## 4. The VSL (Video Sales Letter)

The VSL is the core selling asset behind entry point #4 and the webinar.

**The rule:** you **must speak on camera.** Not a Loom, not a document. You *can* screenshare and use slides — but always **speak to camera first, then start screensharing.** Face-to-camera builds the trust that a faceless deck can't.

**The VSL funnel flow:**
> Ad → Opt-in → VSL → Book Call → Thank You Page → Reminders

### Two VSL structures

**1. Detailed VSL — high-impact, direct (sells with PAIN)**
*Length: 15–20 minutes.*
1. **Hook** — grab attention immediately.
2. **Overview** — tell them what's coming in the next 15 minutes.
3. **Introduction** — who you are, why your agency exists, what you bring.
4. **Success to date** — achievements and past results (credibility).
5. **Pain points** — call out the specific problems your audience faces.
6. **Deliver value** — tangible, relevant insight or solution.
7. **More proof** — case studies, testimonials, additional results.
8. **Pain points (revisited)** — circle back to maintain urgency.
9. **Call to action** — book a call, or BUY.

**2. Simple VSL — professional, broad-appeal (sells with PLEASURE)**
1. **Hook** — strong, engaging opening.
2. **What we do** — clear, high-level overview of services.
3. **How we do it** — your process, briefly, to build confidence.
4. **Who we serve** — the clients you work with (relevance).
5. **How we can help you** — the benefits and results to expect.
6. **Call to action** — reach out or book a call.

> **Which to use:** the **Detailed/PAIN** VSL drives action through urgency and problem-agitation — best for direct-response, warmer audiences ready to be pushed. The **Simple/PLEASURE** VSL is professional and broad — best for a wider, cooler audience or a more premium/considered positioning. (This mirrors the segment-messaging split in *Niche & Target Market*: emotional/urgent for some, aspirational/professional for others.)

## 5. The two conversion → automation paths

What happens *after* the entry point. The whole point is that **nobody falls through the cracks** — converters get nurtured, non-converters get retargeted.

### Path A — VSL landing page → Book a Call
1. VSL page → **Book a call / get in touch.**
2. Track three states:
   - **Didn't book a call** → **Pixel** → **Retargeting ads.**
   - **Didn't show** (booked but no-show) → **Pixel** → **Retargeting ads.**
   - **Showed up** → **Zapier** → **Auto email flow.**

Everyone is **pixelled** so they can be retargeted; the ones who didn't convert get chased by ads, the ones who showed up get dropped into an automated email sequence.

### Path B — Squeeze page → Lead magnet → Email nurture
1. **Squeeze page** (e.g. "Business Toolkit") → capture email.
2. **Email autoresponder fires** → offers access to the free training (delivered on a sales page).
3. A **multi-day training email sequence** drips out — each day's email drives to a landing or sales page:

| Email | Delivered on |
|---|---|
| Day 1 training | Landing page |
| Day 2 training | Sales page |
| Day 3 training | — |
| Day 4 training | Sales page |
| Day 5 training | Sales page |
| Day 6 training | Sales page |

Each email delivers genuine value while routing the lead onto a page that builds toward the offer — value first, sell throughout.

## 6. The tech / automation stack

The funnel runs on a small set of tools:

- **Pixel** (Meta etc.) — track every visitor so they can be retargeted later.
- **Retargeting ads** — re-engage anyone who didn't book or didn't show.
- **Zapier** — trigger automations off conversion events (e.g. "showed up" → start email flow).
- **Email autoresponder** — deliver the lead-magnet nurture sequence automatically.
- **Pages** — main landing page (hub), VSL landing page, squeeze page, sales page, thank-you page, plus booked-call **reminders**.

## Synthesis — how this fits the acquisition system

This file is the **middle layer** of client acquisition — it sits between *generating demand* and *closing the sale*:

1. **Demand** is created by content (*Inbound Leads*) and outreach (*Sign Clients*).
2. **This funnel captures and routes** that demand: traffic → an entry point matched to lead temperature → a VSL or nurture sequence that sells → a booked call.
3. **The sale is closed** by the deck and the two-call sales framework in *Sign Clients Like Never Before*.
4. **Automation** (Pixel, retargeting, Zapier, autoresponders) guarantees every lead is either converted, nurtured, or retargeted — so paid traffic and hard-won attention are never wasted.

**The single biggest idea:** build the funnel so the *only* outcomes are "converts now," "nurtured toward converting," or "retargeted until they convert." There is no fourth outcome where a lead simply disappears — and that's what turns one-time attention into a compounding pipeline.
