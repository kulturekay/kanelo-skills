# Inbound Leads — Personal Branding

> **Client Acquisition · File 1.**
> The earlier guide *Sign Clients Like Never Before* covered **outbound** acquisition — you go find leads, qualify them, and pitch them. This file covers the opposite engine: **inbound.** You build a personal brand and a content machine so that your ideal clients **come to you** already warm, and you nurture them with relationship-first engagement rather than cold pitches.

The core idea: **content earns attention and trust at scale; genuine engagement converts that trust into conversations.** Done right, your IACP discovers you, follows you, sees your expertise repeatedly, and is receptive long before you ever talk price. The engine has four parts: a **pillar-based content strategy**, **platform playbooks**, a **content idea bank**, and a **relationship-first DM/engagement system**.

---

## 1. The foundation — Content Pillars (DEPTH vs REACH)

A personal brand isn't random posting. It's organised around **content pillars** — recurring themes you own. Each pillar serves one of two jobs:

- **DEPTH** — builds trust and converts the people already watching (about you, your business, your expertise). Speaks to your existing audience and your IACP.
- **REACH** — top-of-funnel content designed to attract *new* people who don't know you yet.

You need both: REACH brings strangers in, DEPTH turns them into believers.

### The six-pillar model

| Pillar | Theme | What it is | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — Forever Pillars: What Defines Me** | Personal stories | Your life, experiences, how you live and operate (e.g. humble upbringing, being a young entrepreneur, insecurities, formative struggles) | **DEPTH** |
| **1 — My Agency** | Day-to-day of the business | Behind the scenes, work you're producing, re-sharing client content, announcements | **DEPTH** |
| **2 — Advice to My Target Market** | Tangible advice for your IACP | Practical how-tos and resources aimed squarely at the clients you want (e.g. "content strategy for streamers," "how to edit like [X]") | **DEPTH** |
| **3 — General Entrepreneurship** | TOFU business content | Broad business/entrepreneurship topics that pull reach (building an agency, scaling, the founder journey) | **REACH** |
| **4 — Marketing / Sales** | Tactics you actually use | The marketing and sales methods you run in your own business (how to create content, psychology of content, communication in sales) | **REACH** |
| **5 — Passion / Personal Life** | Your interests & reactions | Reacting to the world across everything you care about (faith, sports, trends/news, health, travel) | **REACH** |

Pillars **0–2 = DEPTH**, pillars **3–5 = REACH**. The example came from a content strategy built explicitly "for building personal brand."

### The DEPTH/REACH ratio

Balance the mix deliberately. The Instagram playbook uses a **7-day cycle of 4 DEPTH + 3 REACH** posts — enough reach to keep new people arriving, enough depth to convert them.

## 2. Platform playbooks

Each platform has its own cadence and content formats. Run them in parallel; many pieces repurpose across platforms (one idea → reel + carousel + tweet + short + community post).

### LinkedIn
**Content types:** advice for your IACP · story-driven posts · infographics · engaging with others.
*(LinkedIn is where written thought-leadership and the weekly newsletter live — see Email below.)*

### Instagram
**Strategy:** 1 post/day · 2–3 organic stories spread through the day.
**Content types:** carousels · reels · images · stories · Q&A.
**Example 7-day calendar (4 depth / 3 reach):**

| Day | Format |
|---|---|
| 1 | Carousel |
| 2 | Reel |
| 3 | Tweet-style image |
| 4 | Image (behind-the-scenes) |
| 5 | Reel |
| 6 | Reel |
| 7 | Carousel / tweet-style |

### Twitter / X
**Strategy:** 2–3 tweets/day · schedule them in advance.
**Content types:** direct statements · reposting your own or others' content into a thread · personal experience · advice · business memes · stories about others · shoutouts · giveaways.

### YouTube
**Strategy:** 1 long video/week · 1 short/day (same shorts as Instagram) · 1 community post (same as LinkedIn/Twitter).
**Content types:** day-in-the-life · deep dives on what you know · general entrepreneurship topics · testimonials/interviews · "how to do X" · "why X works."

### Email (newsletter)
Two newsletters serve two audiences:

**Weekly — to everyone (also posted on LinkedIn), every Monday:**
- Previous week's content
- A written version of your "weekflow"
- Industry updates
- Creator-economy focused
- Built on the audience you're growing via YouTube + LinkedIn

**Monthly — to clients (e.g. a post-month "MGM" recap):**
- Intro from the founder (what you've done + a personal message to the client)
- Global updates (showing off wins)
- Content you made + how your following has grown
- Client highlights (no launches)
- Industry updates (what other platforms are doing)

**Supporting asset:** build a **landing page** to capture subscribers (e.g. "learn how I'm building the creator economy — weekly newsletter").

## 3. The content idea bank ("Reels Doc")

The biggest reason people stop posting is running out of ideas. Fix it with a **running idea bank** — a living document of content ideas filed under each pillar, so you always have something to make. Example structure (organised by pillar/theme):

- **Raw & Real:** failures you've had; why CEOs need a bad day; whether university education matters; advice for Gen-Z/teen entrepreneurs; an early money win; "hire fast, fire fast"; "it's OK for your life to change."
- **Creator Economy:** opinions/predictions about the space (e.g. "NFTs won't go far").
- **Speaking Directly to Entrepreneurs:** turning a side hustle full-time; having to let someone go; an uncomfortable client conversation; your highest-ever paid wage; "people don't become millionaires from day jobs."
- **Life, Not Business:** take the risk; lean into uncertainty.
- **Day-to-Day:** behind-the-scenes of running the business.

> Keep the doc open and add to it whenever an idea strikes. When it's time to post, you're choosing from a list, not staring at a blank page.

## 4. Warming the lead — the engagement & DM system

Content creates inbound interest; engagement converts it. This is the relationship-first counterpart to the cold outreach in *Sign Clients Like Never Before* — slower, softer, and built on trust.

### The daily engagement routine
On Instagram and Twitter/X:
1. **Find your IACP** (the ideal clients you defined in *Niche & Target Market*).
2. **~10 DMs per day.**
3. **~15 minutes per DM** — research and personalise; this is not a blast.
4. **Follow, like, and comment as a real person would** — show up in their world naturally.
5. **Keep interacting over the next 7 days** before expecting anything — familiarity first.

### DM rules
- **Never sell in the first DM.**
- **Never ask for a call in the first DM.**
- **Don't send a video straight up.**

### 🚩 Red-flag phrases to avoid
"Done-for-you" / DFY · DWY · "pay-on-results" · "we will pay you…" · guarantees · "get X results in Y days." These scream automated pitch and kill trust.

### 🟢 Non-negotiables
- **Use their name.**
- **Make it obviously not fake or automated.**
- **Actually put effort in.**

### The four DM frameworks

| Framework | When to use | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Open Loop** | Start a value-led conversation | *"Hey {name}, seen {brand}, love {relevant detail}. I looked through {problem} and think it could improve by {value}. What's your current plan on {problem}?"* |
| **2 — Example Work** | Offer proof without pitching | Same opener + *"I'm X and do this for XYZ. Got my team to make some examples — what's the best email to send them to?"* |
| **3 — Lead Magnet** | Give value to earn a reply | Same opener + *"I put together a simple cheatsheet (free) — you can get it here {link}."* |
| **4 — Just Conversation** | Pure relationship-building | *"Hey {name}, have a question — {a genuinely relevant question that starts a conversation}."* |

Every framework leads with *them*, gives value, and ends with a low-friction question — never a sell.

## Synthesis — how the inbound engine works

The four parts feed each other in a loop:

1. **Pillars** (DEPTH + REACH) give you a strategy, not random posts — REACH content pulls strangers in, DEPTH content converts them into trust.
2. **Platform playbooks** distribute that strategy at the right cadence across LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter/X, YouTube, and email — repurposing one idea many ways.
3. **The idea bank** keeps the machine fuelled so you never stall.
4. **The engagement & DM system** warms your IACP through genuine, no-sell interaction until they're receptive.

The result: your ideal client finds you through REACH content, follows you, absorbs your DEPTH content and expertise, sees you in their DMs and comments as a real person — and by the time a conversation about working together happens, **they already trust you.** That's the whole advantage of inbound over outbound: the lead arrives pre-sold.

> **Inbound vs outbound:** *Sign Clients Like Never Before* is the outbound engine — you initiate, qualify, pitch, close. This file is the inbound engine — you attract, nurture, and let warm leads come to you. The strongest agencies run both at once; inbound lowers your cost of acquisition over time and makes every outbound touch land warmer.
